# Service tier capability map

The workflow defaults to \`elite\`. The tier setting controls the market-universe limit; commercial billing and human support remain outside the workflow.

## Essential

- one selected market;
- deterministic Entry, TP1-TP4 and SL;
- AI explanation;
- basic balanced risk profile;
- instant notification through a configured channel.

## Professional

- up to three selected markets;
- continuous 15-minute scans and daily briefing;
- Telegram and WhatsApp connectors;
- signal lifecycle updates;
- economic-news impact analysis;
- opportunity ranking and priority delivery.

## Elite

- no artificial workflow market limit; provider quotas still apply;
- no artificial workflow signal limit;
- five-strategy ensemble;
- conservative, balanced or aggressive profile;
- secured personal market-query endpoint;
- detailed bilingual reports and tweet summary;
- VIP ranking for the strongest confirmed opportunities;
- signal lifecycle and rolling TP1/SL performance;
- new capabilities can be added without changing the public webhook contract.

Early feature access, onboarding and dedicated VIP support are service operations, not claims made by the analysis code.
